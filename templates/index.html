How it works

Four simple steps.

1. Paste in the posting

Copy the job title and description from the listing you want to check.

2. Add company details (optional)

If you have the company's name, website, or contact email, add them for a fuller check.

3. Click Analyze

The trained model reviews the posting in a few seconds.

4. Review your results

See the risk score, warning signs, and company verification side by side.

What this system checks

AI detection

A trained machine learning model reviews the posting text for common fraud patterns.

Risk analysis

A 0–100 risk score and a Low / Medium / High risk level, with plain-English reasons.

Company verification

Simple checks on the company name, website, and email for mismatches or missing details.

Analysis history

Every analysis is saved so you can look back at past results anytime.
